Connect live data sources

Finnhub loads company earnings. Financial Modeling Prep loads U.S. macro events such as CPI, PPI, jobs, claims, and PMI when included in your plan. Census, BEA and Federal Reserve schedules load from official sources through the local server.

Keys are stored in this browser's local storage. Finnhub is called from this browser; the FMP key is sent to the local server and then to FMP. Economic-calendar access may depend on your FMP plan. Do not use on a shared computer.
